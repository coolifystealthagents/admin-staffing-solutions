# Admin Staffing Solutions — V3 Design Direction

## Positioning
A specialist administrative staffing site for business owners, operations leaders, and executive teams. The experience should feel like a calm, decisive command desk rather than a generic recruiting marketplace.

## Visual system
- **Theme:** light, editorial utility
- **Base:** cobalt `#1738c7`
- **Action:** safety orange `#ff6b1a`
- **Canvas:** warm white `#fbfbf7`
- **Ink:** near-black `#121317`
- **Shapes:** squared ticket, register, and ledger forms; minimal radius
- **Typography:** assertive grotesk display type paired with readable sans-serif body type
- **Density:** operational and information-rich without crowding
- **Motion:** limited to navigation feedback and the review rail; reduced-motion safe

## Signature element
The delegation-control board is the recurring visual device. It makes queue, owner, evidence, decision boundary, and escalation visible rather than illustrating work with generic software dashboards.

## Image direction
Commercial pages use distinct editorial workflow illustrations tied to each route. Editorial pages use unique evidence-led covers keyed to the article subject. First-party team photography is reserved for parent-company proof and clearly labeled as such.

## Navigation and conversion
The persistent primary navigation contains exactly **Home**, **Services**, and **Contact Us**. About, Blog, Research, Privacy, and Terms remain reachable through secondary footer navigation so all public routes are preserved. The Contact Us first fold pairs title and compact review proof on the left with the complete, unchanged lead form on the right.

## Accessibility
Keyboard access, visible focus, semantic headings, non-interactive duplicated marquee content, descriptive media alternatives, high-contrast footer attribution, and reduced-motion behavior are mandatory.

## Design settings
- Variance: 8
- Motion: 3
- Density: 7
- Evolution mode: evolve
